title: About me
---

I'm a cognitive neuroscientist. I use neuroimaging — fMRI, MEG and EEG — together with videogames, art and
brain-machine interfaces to explore intelligence, biological or artificial, and to understand how digital media
shape our minds.

I did my PhD at Université de Montréal with Karim Jerbi and Lune Bellec, on attention, flow and the neuroimaging of
videogame play. I then worked as a postdoctoral fellow on the Courtois NeuroMod project, building a large Super Mario
Bros. fMRI dataset, and spent a few months in Rui Ponte Costa's Neural & Machine Learning group at the University of
Oxford. I am now a postdoctoral researcher at Boston College.

On the edges of the lab, I co-founded the art-science collective [Kairos ~ Hive](https://kairos-hive.org), where we
turn brain and body signals into cinema, music and stories — and I DJ as Xanarelle.

I care about open, free, diverse, accessible and equitable science: most of what I build is open source, and the
datasets I work on are shared with the community.
