Before videogames became my research topic, they were — and still are — a hobby, competitive ones included: with my
team I finished third of the Ligue Cyber Espoir Overwatch tournament in 2017. LoLTDA is a little analyzer of League of
Legends tournament drafts that I wrote in 2019.
