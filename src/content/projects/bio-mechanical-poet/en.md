The Bio-Mechanical Poet listens to your brain. Peaks in the EEG spectrum are mapped onto chemical elements, planets and
colours, which in turn steer a poetry-writing language model, an image diffusion model and a generative music engine.
Presented as a paper and live demo at the International Conference on Computational Creativity (ICCC 2024).
