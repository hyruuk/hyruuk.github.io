- **Numina** (2021) — an audiovisual neurofeedback interface to explore mental states through generative universes,
  shown at Montréal Connecte and documented by Hexagram.
- **In Outself** (CoSyne 2023) — a neurofeedback audiovisual performance with a DJ and a VJ.
- **Festivals** — live demonstrations at Portail (2018), Good Vibes Diskodissey (2022) and Groove and Bass (2023).
- **Consulting** — on biosignals in artistic creation, for example with Moment Factory (2019).
