The P300 is a brain response to a rare event you are waiting for. Flash the squares of a chessboard, and the one you
attend to evokes a P300 that an EEG decoder can pick up — enough to select a piece and a move. This prototype, built
with locked-in patients in mind, includes a keyboard demo mode to try the interface without a headset.
