*V F C* is a 98-minute adaptive horror feature directed by Charles Stéphane Roy and starring Elisapie Isaac. Each
spectator wears an EEG headset and bone-conduction headphones: at 13 "gates" in the film, their brain activity decides
which branch of the sound design they hear — 8,192 possible soundtracks, one per mind.

With Antoine Bellemare-Pepin, I designed the neurofeedback side: which brain signals to read, how to make them robust
in a dark room full of strangers, and how to map them onto the sound branches. The system was tested with about a
hundred viewers over two years. The film premiered at the Festival du nouveau cinéma (2023) and travelled to Cannes
NEXT, FIVARS (two awards), Laval Virtual and the Cinémathèque québécoise.

We presented the project at ISEA 2024 in Brisbane, and the method is described in a published US patent application.
