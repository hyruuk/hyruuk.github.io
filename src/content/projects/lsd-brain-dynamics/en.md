Psychedelics are a powerful way to perturb consciousness — and a test bench for theories of how brain dynamics give
rise to experience. In two studies led by V. Subramani, we analysed MEG data recorded under LSD:

- LSD shifts cortical activity towards **faster rhythms** and a **higher fractal dimension** — more complex signals —
  and the study also examines how the drug interacts with listening to music (*Communications Biology*, 2026).
- Viewed through connectome harmonics, brain activity under LSD is **less constrained by the brain's structural
  wiring** (preprint, 2026).
