TideRipper started as a personal itch: my playlists live on streaming services, but a DJ needs a consistent local
library — the same format everywhere, matched loudness, tempo and key analysed. TideRipper does the boring part in one
pipeline, with releases for macOS, Windows and Linux (AGPL-3.0).
