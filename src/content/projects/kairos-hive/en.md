Kairos ~ Hive is a transdisciplinary collective — *harvesting synchronicities* — where science, art and technology
meet. We build bio-adaptive artworks and open tools that let brain and body signals shape images, sounds and stories,
and we offer consulting and education around them.

The collective's projects include ReTiNA-Cinema, the Bio-Mechanical Poet, the goofi-pipe biofeedback framework and
contributions to the adaptive film V F C. In 2026 we published *Bio-Adaptive Mythologies*, an essay imagining
storytelling systems driven by breath and heartbeat.
