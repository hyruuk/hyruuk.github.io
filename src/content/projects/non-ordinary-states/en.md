Some people can voluntarily enter a non-ordinary state of consciousness: a self-induced cognitive trance. This study,
a collaboration between Liège and Montréal led by V. Oswald and O. Gosseries, measured the autonomic nervous system
during trance. Heart and breathing dynamics change markedly — enough for classifiers to decode the state from them.
