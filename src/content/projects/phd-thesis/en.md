*Du flux cérébral au flow psychologique : vers une étude de la fluctuation des états attentionnels par la
neuroimagerie du jeu vidéo* — supervised by Karim Jerbi and Lune Bellec.

The thesis asks how we could study states like flow — deep, effortless absorption — with brain imaging, and argues
that videogames are the right tool for it. It brings together three pieces of work:

1. an MRI- and MEG-compatible videogame controller,
2. a method to automatically annotate game events for fMRI analysis (Shinobi),
3. the MEG correlates of moment-to-moment fluctuations in sustained attention.
