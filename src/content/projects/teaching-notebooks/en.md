Teaching is how I check that I really understand something. Over the years: teaching assistant in neuroscience methods
and quantitative analysis at Université de Montréal, peer instructor at Brainhack School, workshops on MNE-Python and
scikit-learn (NeuroQAM summer school) and on scientific programming (Brainhack School 2025), and an educational session
on videogames and reinforcement learning for Neuro-AI at MAIN 2025. The neurotuto notebooks collect some of this
material.
