- **Divergent creativity in humans and LLMs.** We compared many large language models with large samples of people on
  a word-association test of divergent thinking and on creative writing (haiku, film synopses, flash fiction), and
  measured how temperature and prompting change the results. Models now beat many humans — but not the most creative
  ones (*Scientific Reports*, 2026).
- **Pareidolia as a sign of creativity.** Creative people see more shapes in fractal noise, and see them faster —
  pareidolia as a window onto creative perception (*iScience*, 2022).
- I also talked about these questions at the TimeWorld conference on AI in Montréal (2022): *is creativity the
  prerogative of biological intelligences?*
