When I'm not analysing brain rhythms, I play with dance-floor ones. As **Xanarelle** I mostly play techno and psytech,
house, dubstep and drum & bass — most recently closing CoSyne 2025 in Mont-Tremblant, back-to-back with Mat Moebius.

My DJ library is built with [TideRipper](/projects/tideripper/), a tool I wrote to turn playlists into a ready-to-mix
collection. Want a taste? Press the little wave in the corner of the page: it plays random tracks from my liked songs.
