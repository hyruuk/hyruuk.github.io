CoCo Brain Channel was my first brain-music machine, built in Karim Jerbi's CoCo Lab with Antoine Bellemare-Pepin. A
consumer EEG headset streams brain activity into a generative music engine built in PureData: features of the signal
modulate the harmony and rhythm of a bank of oscillators, producing a continuous soundscape that follows the
listener's mental state.

It went from a first public demo at the Printemps numérique (Musée McCord, 2018) to a live performance with DJ and VJ
at OHBM 2019 in Rome, with stops at NeuroMusic, Neuroinformatics, CogSci and several festivals.
